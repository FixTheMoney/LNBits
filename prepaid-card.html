Prepaid Lightning Cards

Create funded sub-wallets users can spend from — reusable until balance runs out or you deactivate

How prepaid cards work
Each card is a LNbits sub-wallet funded from your main wallet. The cardholder gets a wallet access URL — they open it in a browser to see their balance and scan a QR to pay any Lightning merchant (standard BOLT11 invoice or LNURL-pay).

The card stays spendable until the balance hits zero or you deactivate it here. You can also top up a card at any time. The card URL never changes — the same link works forever (until deactivated). No app install required for the cardholder — just a browser.

⚠️ Deactivating a card
Deactivating deletes the sub-wallet from LNbits. Any remaining balance is lost (LNbits does not return funds from deleted sub-wallets to the parent wallet via the API). Always top up only what you are willing to distribute. To reclaim funds before deactivating, create a withdrawal from the sub-wallet first, or send the cardholder the remaining balance manually.
